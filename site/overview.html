What “live” means

This presentation reads the published research and work register when opened, restored, or returned to view. It requests a fresh copy, and every excerpt links back to its canonical source. There is no separately maintained slide narrative.

“Latest” means the latest published revision returned by this site—not unpublished edits. Browser, network, or CDN delays can still affect delivery. The publication timestamp, revision, and last successful check are shown above. A failed refresh is explicit and hides unverified content.
